The problem is rarely clinical skill.

It's the absence of structured business systems around the practice. These are the patterns we see most often.

Low footfall

Not enough patients walking through the door on a consistent basis.

Poor conversion

Phone and walk-in enquiries that never turn into confirmed appointments.

Weak retention

Patients who don't return for follow-ups, maintenance or recurring care.

Limited referrals

Satisfied patients who never quite turn into word-of-mouth growth.

Visibility gaps

A Google Business Profile and local search presence that underperform.

Competition

Nearby practices simply executing the operational basics better.

The Aleph Practice Growth Framework™

Every engagement — whether a single assessment or an ongoing partnership — moves through the same four disciplined stages.

Understand

A deep dive into your clinic's infrastructure, service mix, patient demographics, location and baseline performance — before anything is prescribed.

Diagnose

A rigorous evaluation across five dimensions, so recommendations are rooted in evidence rather than guesswork.

  • Visibility
  • Acquisition
  • Conversion
  • Retention
  • Competitive landscape

Strategize

A customized, prioritized, step-by-step growth roadmap built around what your diagnosis actually shows — not a generic playbook.

Improve

Hands-on support with execution, tracking the key performance indicators that matter, and refining tactics as real data comes in.

Who we help

We currently focus on two specialties, with more outpatient specialties joining as the framework expands.

Dentists (BDS)

Running a 1–3 chair practice and want steadier footfall

Most dental clinics we assess aren't short on clinical quality — they're short on systems for local visibility and referrals.

  • Local search and Google Business Profile ranking
  • Building a repeatable, systematic referral pipeline
  • Turning first-time patients into long-term retention
Cosmetologists & Aesthetic Clinicians

Strong interest, but enquiries aren't converting

An active Instagram presence often masks a conversion problem — enquiries come in, but too few turn into booked appointments.

  • Enquiry-to-appointment conversion process
  • Turning social proof into scheduled consultations
  • Standing out in a crowded, aesthetics-heavy local market

Dermatology, physiotherapy, ophthalmology and other outpatient specialties are next. If that's you, we'd still like to hear from you.

    About Dr. Wany

    "Being an excellent clinician and running a sustainable practice are two different skill sets — and almost no one teaches the second one."

    Dr. Wany founded Aleph Medico after watching clinically excellent colleagues struggle with the business side of running a practice: inconsistent footfall, enquiries that went nowhere, and no structured way to work out why.

    That gap — between clinical skill and business systems — is what Aleph Medico exists to close. Not with generic marketing packages, but with a framework grounded in how clinical practices actually operate day to day.

    Today, Dr. Wany works directly with independent dentists, cosmetologists and aesthetic clinicians to diagnose what's holding their practice back, and to build growth plans that are specific to their patients, their city and their competitive landscape.
