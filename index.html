The real problem

Low footfall is often only the symptom.

Clinicians can spend months changing ads, posting more content or adding discounts without first understanding where the practice is actually losing potential patients.

Not enough enquiries

People may not be discovering the practice, understanding its value, or finding a reason to choose it.

Enquiries don't convert

Interest can disappear between the first message, the front desk, the consultation and the booking decision.

Patients don't return

A practice can work hard to acquire patients while leaving retention, recall and experience underdeveloped.

Competition feels stronger

Competitors may simply be communicating their value more clearly and creating a more consistent patient journey.
