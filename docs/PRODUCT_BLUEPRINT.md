# Aleph Production Blueprint

## Product Definition

Aleph is a practice growth intelligence platform for independent clinicians. It helps clinicians become discoverable, trusted, chosen, and commercially stable without violating healthcare ethics.

## Conversion Architecture

1. Homepage establishes pain and positioning.
2. Diagnostic identifies growth leakage.
3. Report gives personalized insight.
4. Lead is scored for follow-up.
5. Admin dashboard supports sales action.
6. Pricing and solution pages convert intent into packages.

## Locked Sprint Sequence

- Sprint 0: Foundation
- Sprint 1: Homepage and brand system
- Sprint 2: Diagnostic
- Sprint 3: Backend/admin
- Sprint 4: Pricing and segment pages
- Sprint 5: Automation/resources
- Sprint 6: QA/deployment hardening
